The sourcing evidence gap

The physical product moves. Its evidence often breaks apart.

Origin, quality, custody and processing records live across suppliers, spreadsheets and documents. Buyers are left reconstructing proof after the transaction, when the evidence is hardest to verify.

01

Origin disappears downstream

Farm and plot information is often reduced to a country, region or supplier claim before the commodity reaches a buyer.

02

Processing breaks provenance

Aggregation, blending and transformation can separate an output lot from the records of its contributing origins.

03

Due diligence becomes paperwork

Teams spend time collecting documents without a dependable link between the evidence, the physical lot and commercial decisions.

Multi-hop traceability

Processing should not erase where a product came from.

AgriTrace follows source-lot relationships recursively, allowing a processed lot to resolve to its contributing farm-level origins through multiple transformation steps.
