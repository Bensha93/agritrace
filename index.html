Know Exactly Where Your Food Comes From

AgriTrace is a global agrifood sourcing intelligence platform. We connect verified farm-level production records with commercial demand; so manufacturers, brokers, and food buyers can source commodities with confidence, transparency, and full traceability.

🌾 Farmers — All Scales 🔗 Brokers 🏭 Manufacturers & Buyers 🌍 Worldwide

The Problem We Are Solving

Sourcing decisions are still built on trust, not evidence

European food manufacturers and buyers are under increasing pressure to prove where their commodities come from; but the supply chain cannot yet provide that proof.

📄

Unverifiable Paper Records

Sourcing decisions rely on documents and verbal assurances that cannot be independently confirmed at any stage of the supply chain.

🔍

No Farm-Level Visibility

Buyers have no view into how, where, or by whom a commodity was grown; only what an intermediary chooses to report.

⚖️

Rising Regulatory Pressure

EU Farm to Fork, CSDDD, and the EU Deforestation Regulation all demand traceable, documented sourcing chains that most businesses cannot yet produce.

🚫

Excluded Producers

Farmers of all sizes — without structured records or market access tools; cannot participate in higher-value, verified commercial supply chains.

How It Works

Three rings. One verified supply chain.

AgriTrace connects the full supply chain; from the farm to the manufacturer; through a single, auditable, immutable record system.

1

🌾 Farmers — Every Scale

  • Register farms and plots with soil and location data
  • Record all inputs: seeds, fertilisers, labour, transport
  • Track crop development and harvest outcomes
  • Log costs, revenue, and profit per farming round
  • Photo records of crop growth stages
  • AI-assisted guidance for pests and crop issues
2

🔗 Brokers

  • Organise farms into structured farming rounds
  • Aggregate supply from any size of farm operation
  • Match verified supply to manufacturer specifications
  • Manage sourcing commitments with documented evidence
  • Build a track record of supply reliability
3

🏭 Manufacturers & Buyers

  • Define commodity specifications, quality, and volume
  • Source from verified, documented supply chains
  • Trace commodities to specific farms and plots
  • Access production history, inputs, and yield performance
  • Satisfy regulatory due diligence with auditable records
